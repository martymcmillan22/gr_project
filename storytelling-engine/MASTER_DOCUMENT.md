# ⚙️ **SECTION 4 — ENGINE SCHEMA (Story‑Telling Engine v1.0 Rules)**

## **Core Structure for Every Story Entry**

### **1. Talking Points**
- 3–5 high-level hooks
- Designed for pitch/pitch deck
- Audience-agnostic ("What makes this story compelling?")

### **2. Title**
- Final working title
- May evolve during development
- Locked at Entry completion

### **3. Core Concept**
- Single-sentence essence
- Thematic pillars (2–3 themes)
- Unique selling proposition (USP)

### **4. Synopsis**
- 200–300 words
- Act structure overview (beginning, middle, end)
- Tone and genre signals

### **5. Chapter Structure**
- 36–60 chapters total
- Chapter titles + 1-line descriptions
- Natural pacing/break points
- Dynamic scene distribution

### **6. Dynamic Navigation (DN)**
- Scene-to-scene connectors
- Suggested reading paths
- Cross-references to other entries
- Interactive branching (optional)

### **7. Soundtrack Timeline**
- Chapter-by-chapter music recommendations
- Mood/tempo guidance
- Artist/track references
- Optional Spotify/Apple Music links

### **8. Character Profiles**
- Main characters (5–10)
- Secondary characters (10–15)
- Role in narrative arc
- Appearance, personality, backstory (brief)

### **9. Research File**
- Source materials used
- Fact-checking notes
- Historical/contextual references
- Expert interviews (if applicable)

### **10. Future Timeline**
- Planned sequels or spin-offs
- Expanded universe notes
- Author's long-term vision

### **11. Table of Contents**
- Full chapter list with page numbers
- Part/section breaks
- Cross-reference index

### **12. Bibliography**
- Sources cited
- Recommended reading
- Inspirational works
- Academic references

### **13. Anti‑Plagiarism Section**
- Original concept statement
- Unique elements declaration
- Comparison to similar works
- Originality certification
