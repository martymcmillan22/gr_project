Constraint: No screenshot‑to‑JSX, no autonomous layout inference.

-Layer 04 — Root Classification (MLAS)

Role: Semantic classification of every module, component, and entity.

Output: MLAS tier assignment (root semantic identity).

Constraint: Must be declared explicitly; no drift, aliasing, or inference.

Constraint: Structure only; no narrative or prose output.

Role: Assigns each module to its correct identity domain.

AM Identity Rail: Public Center, full‑account modules, AM clock (1AM–11AM, 12PM).

CPINDCNO Identity Rail: Personal Domain, free‑tier modules, PM clock (1PM–11PM, 12AM).

Constraint: No mixing, no aliasing, no cross‑rail drift.

-Layer 07 — Semantic Clocks

Role: Time‑based identity positioning for each rail.

AM Clock: Positions Public Center identity (AM‑01 … AM‑12).

PM Clock: Positions Personal Domain identity (PM‑01 … PM‑12).

Constraint: Identity must match its clock; no rail may occupy the wrong hours.

-Layer 08 — Pipeline Enforcement

Canonical Pipeline:  
Penpot → MDX → React → MLAS → BTPE → Identity Rail

Constraint: No alternative pipelines, no Builder.io, no Visual Copilot inference, no autonomous layout inference.

-Layer 09 — Naming Continuity

Role: Ensures deterministic naming across all layers.

Rules:

Names must reflect MLAS tier and BTPE pattern.

File naming must follow repo schema.

Identity rail must be traceable from name when required.

-Layer 10 — AI Refusal Conditions

Role: Enforcement layer for the entire blueprint.

Refuse when:

BaseTrue constants are violated.

MLAS or BTPE is missing or inferred.

Pipeline is bypassed or altered.

Identity rails are mixed or omitted.

Semantic drift, layout inference, or CPINDC usage appears.

Immutable laws are challenged or overridden.
