# Incubation Ecosystem: Executive One-Pager

## Vision
Turn unstructured ideas into launch-ready businesses through a governed three-phase pipeline:
1. Capture (RAW)
2. Incubate (SEED)
3. Launch (BUSINESS)

## Why This Matters
- Scales cleanly across 64 industries without hard-coded logic.
- Preserves quality with lifecycle gates and auditability.
- Converts creativity into measurable business outcomes.

## North Star Outcomes
1. Every idea is captured with industry alignment and traceable identity.
2. Every seed is validated with industry-specific structured metadata.
3. Every business launch passes readiness controls before execution.

## Phase Summary

### Phase 1: Capture (RAW)
- Dynamic Industry Taxonomy (DB-driven, rename/add with no code change)
- Unified Idea Capture API
- Similarity-based identity mapping for cross-reference candidates

Success signal:
- High ingestion reliability with low invalid-industry submissions

### Phase 2: Incubate (SEED)
- JSONB metadata engine for industry-specific requirements
- Snapshot/version trail from RAW to SEED
- Collaborative polish workflows with ownership integrity

Success signal:
- Higher RAW->SEED conversion with lower rework due to missing requirements

### Phase 3: Launch (BUSINESS)
- Seed->Business transformation service
- Lifecycle readiness gate (mandatory metadata enforcement)
- Read-only cross-reference API across industries

Success signal:
- Increased SEED->BUSINESS conversion with reduced launch defects

## Platform Principles
- Dynamic over hard-coded
- Service-object business logic
- Read-only cross-industry intelligence
- Traceability by design
- Secure and role-aware collaboration

## KPI Framework
1. Conversion Funnel
- RAW->SEED conversion rate
- SEED->BUSINESS conversion rate

2. Quality
- Readiness gate pass rate
- Missing-field failure rate before launch

3. Performance
- Cross-reference API latency (p95)
- Industry lookup response time

4. Governance
- Lifecycle transitions with complete audit trail
- Contributor attribution coverage

## Slide-Friendly Narrative
- Capture ideas with precision.
- Incubate with structured intelligence.
- Launch with validated readiness.
